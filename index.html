Decks

Pick a deck, or all of them. Each session mixes your due cards with up to 10 new ones, in random order. Random card gives you a practice run on any card in the deck without touching its schedule. Get a card right first try and it comes back later (4 hours, 1 day, 3 days, a week, 2 weeks, a month, 3 months). Miss it and it comes back in 4 hours.
